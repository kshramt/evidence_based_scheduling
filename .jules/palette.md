## 2026-06-13 - Add ARIA labels to icon-only buttons\n**Learning:** When adding ARIA labels to widely-used icon-only components like `btn-icon`, it's important to provide both `aria-label` for screen readers and `title` for native tooltip hover support to ensure both accessibility and general usability are enhanced.\n**Action:** Standardize the approach of including both `aria-label` and `title` whenever creating or modifying icon-only interactive elements.
