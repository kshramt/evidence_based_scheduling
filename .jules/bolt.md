## 2024-03-26 - O(N) Re-render Cascades in List Item Components
**Learning:** List item components in this codebase (like `QueueEntry` inside `react-virtuoso` lists, or `MobileQueueNode` inside mapped arrays) that receive primitive props (`node_id`, `index`) can cause unnecessary O(N) global re-renders during state updates or scrolling. This is a codebase-specific architectural bottleneck where mapping child nodes without memoization defeats the efficiency of virtualized lists and causes performance degradation when the queue size grows.
**Action:** When creating or modifying components that serve as rows or nodes in lists (virtualized or standard mapped arrays), explicitly wrap them in `React.memo` to ensure they only re-render when their primitive props change or their internal selectors are triggered, preventing full-list re-render cascades.
